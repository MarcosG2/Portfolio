# Product — Marcos Guerrero portfolio

## What this is

A bilingual (ES/EN) personal portfolio for **Marcos Guerrero**, a full-stack developer. Visitors explore projects, skills, experience, and contact options.

## Audience

- Recruiters and hiring managers scanning proof of work
- Peers and potential collaborators
- Clients evaluating freelance fit

## Success

- Understand who Marcos is and what he builds within one viewport
- Reach live demos, GitHub repos, or CV download without hunting
- Send a message or follow a social link

## Constraints

- Static SPA (React + Vite), deployable on Vercel or GitHub Pages
- Content is truthful: WIP/planned projects are labeled, not oversold
- Bilingual copy; language preference persists locally

## Out of scope (for now)

- Backend beyond form relay (Formspree)
- CMS or admin panel
